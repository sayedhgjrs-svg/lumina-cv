Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how Barcode Studio handles information when you use the website. Update this policy if your hosting, analytics, advertising, or data practices change.

Information you enter

The text or number you enter into the generator is processed locally in your browser to create the barcode. It is not submitted to our application server by the generator. Avoid entering sensitive or confidential information into any barcode if you do not want that information to be readable by others who scan it.

Website operations

Your web host may process routine technical information, such as an IP address, browser details, and request time, to deliver and protect the website. The exact information retained depends on the hosting provider and its configuration. We do not operate an account system on this website.

Third-party libraries

The generator loads barcode libraries from a third-party content delivery network (CDN). When your browser requests those files, the CDN may receive standard connection information. The generator may not work if those files cannot be loaded.

Advertising

This website includes clearly marked spaces reserved for advertisements. These are placeholders and do not themselves load advertisements. If an advertising provider is added, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies. This policy and any required consent controls should be updated to reflect the provider, applicable laws, and the actual configuration before ads are enabled.

Cookies and analytics

The generator does not intentionally set cookies. The hosting provider or any future analytics or advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies. Confirm your deployment configuration and update this section if those services are enabled.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website changes. For questions, visit the Contact page for the available contact route.
