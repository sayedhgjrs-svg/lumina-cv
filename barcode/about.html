ABOUT US

A simple tool for everyday barcodes

Barcode Studio is a free online tool for creating QR codes and a selection of commonly used 1D barcodes. We built it to make barcode creation straightforward: choose a format, enter your content, customize the appearance, and download your image.

Free and easy to access

You can use the generator without creating an account. There are no paid features or usage limits imposed by this page. Some barcode formats have specific content rules, so always confirm that your chosen format is accepted by your scanner, product listing, or industry.

Privacy by design

Barcode generation takes place in your web browser. The text or number you enter is not sent to our application server for barcode generation. See our Privacy Policy for more details about website hosting, third-party libraries, and advertising space.

Formats and compatibility

Available formats include QR Code, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128 variants, Code 39, ITF-14, ITF, Codabar, MSI, and Pharmacode. Availability does not guarantee that a format is suitable for every use case.
